Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

STKS Tools is used only by its owner, to back up the owner's own Google account data.

  • What it accesses: the owner's own Google Drive, Google Photos and Gmail, and only when the owner runs it.
  • Where data goes: only to the owner's own computer and drives. Nothing is sent to any other person, company or server.
  • Sharing and selling: no data is shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Removing access: access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
